# Canonical Model

Norvia's own message format ([GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md#integration)): one JSON Schema per business document or event, named with the glossary's terms and versioned in the file name. Partner formats are mapped to and from these at the edges. Changes to a published version are additive; a breaking change is a new version.

| Schema | What it is |
|---|---|
| [transport-order.v1](transport-order.v1.schema.json) | A Transport Order, as submitted to the TMS intake API and as carried on Transport Order events |
| [transport-order-accepted.v1](transport-order-accepted.v1.schema.json) | Event: the TMS accepted a Transport Order |
| [transport-order-rejected.v1](transport-order-rejected.v1.schema.json) | Event: the TMS rejected a booking, with every reason |
| [transport-order-rated.v1](transport-order-rated.v1.schema.json) | Event: the TMS rated an accepted Transport Order, with its Freight Charge |
| [transport-order-rating-failed.v1](transport-order-rating-failed.v1.schema.json) | Event: the TMS couldn't rate an accepted Transport Order, with the failure code and message |
| [transport-order-billable.v1](transport-order-billable.v1.schema.json) | Event: the TMS decided a Transport-only order is Billable (Delivered, Rated, with a current Proof of delivery), with its frozen Freight Charge, the Shipper reference, the Shipment number, the delivery time, whether it was released without a Proof of delivery, and the correlation IDs of its intake and of the transaction that made it Billable; sample messages are in [samples/transport-order-billable](samples/transport-order-billable) |
| [billable-line.v1](billable-line.v1.schema.json) | Document: the ERP's record of one Billable Transport Order, what `TransportOrderBillable` carries kept as the TMS froze it, with the line's status (Waiting until an Invoice run invoices it) and when the ERP recorded it |
| [billable-line-recording.v1](billable-line-recording.v1.schema.json) | Document: the ERP billing API's answer to recording a Billable line: the line, and whether it is a repeat of one already recorded |
| [carrier-cost-record.v1](carrier-cost-record.v1.schema.json) | Document: the ERP's record of one Carrier cost, what `CarrierCostIncurred` carries kept as the TMS froze it (charge lines and total, Carrier Rate Card version or Spot with the Planner's remark), with its status (Open until a Carrier invoice line is Matched or Accepted against it) and when the ERP recorded it |
| [carrier-cost-recording.v1](carrier-cost-recording.v1.schema.json) | Document: the ERP billing API's answer to recording a Carrier cost: the Carrier cost, and whether it is a repeat of one already recorded |
| [shipper-invoice.v1](shipper-invoice.v1.schema.json) | Document: a Shipper invoice, the ERP's invoice to one Shipper over its Billable lines, with its number, date, Shipper, one line per Transport Order (with the charge lines and line total), the totals without VAT, the VAT and with VAT, and its status (Issued, Sent with the time the Shipper invoice Logic App reported, or Not delivered with the technical cause); the number, lines and totals are final once issued |
| [shipper-invoice-issued.v1](shipper-invoice-issued.v1.schema.json) | Event: the ERP issued a Shipper invoice, carrying the whole invoice with its lines, totals and Shipper, under a correlation ID created for the invoice; sample messages are in [samples/shipper-invoice-issued](samples/shipper-invoice-issued) |
| [shipper-invoice-not-delivered.v1](shipper-invoice-not-delivered.v1.schema.json) | Event: a Shipper invoice never reached the Shipper; the Shipper invoice Logic App reported it with the technical cause, under the invoice's correlation ID. Monitoring consumes it |
| [credit-note.v1](credit-note.v1.schema.json) | Document: a Credit note, Finance's correction of one line of an issued Shipper invoice, with its number (`CN-<year>-<6 digits>`), date, Shipper, the invoice and line it credits, the amount without VAT, the VAT and with VAT, and the required reason; final once issued |
| [credit-note-issued.v1](credit-note-issued.v1.schema.json) | Event: Finance issued a Credit note, carrying the whole Credit note with the invoice and line credited, amounts, reason and Shipper, under a correlation ID created for the Credit note; sample messages are in [samples/credit-note-issued](samples/credit-note-issued) |
| [shipment-planned.v1](shipment-planned.v1.schema.json) | Event: a Planner assigned a Transport Order to Own fleet or a Carrier, and the TMS made its Planned Shipment |
| [shipment-reassigned.v1](shipment-reassigned.v1.schema.json) | Event: a Planner reassigned a Planned Shipment to Own fleet or another Carrier, with who carried it before |
| [shipment-released.v1](shipment-released.v1.schema.json) | Event: a Planner released a Planned Shipment, handing it over with its Transport Order's addresses, windows and goods lines, who carries it, and its Carrier booking number and Carrier cost for a Carrier Shipment |
| [carrier-cost.v1](carrier-cost.v1.schema.json) | Document: what Norvia pays a Carrier for one Carrier booking, calculated at release from the Carrier Rate Card, or entered as a Spot price, and frozen, with its charge lines, total, source and the time it was calculated; carried by `ShipmentReleased` |
| [carrier-booking-answer.v1](carrier-booking-answer.v1.schema.json) | Command: a Carrier's confirmation or rejection of a Requested Carrier booking, as the Carrier-answer Logic App sends it to the TMS Carrier booking API's answer operation |
| [carrier-booking-confirmed.v1](carrier-booking-confirmed.v1.schema.json) | Event: a Carrier confirmed a Requested Carrier booking with its own booking reference and the total of the Carrier cost it accepted, making the Shipment Booked |
| [carrier-booking-rejected.v1](carrier-booking-rejected.v1.schema.json) | Event: a Carrier rejected a Requested Carrier booking with a reason, sending the Shipment back to Planned |
| [carrier-booking-not-delivered.v1](carrier-booking-not-delivered.v1.schema.json) | Event: a Carrier booking request never reached the Carrier, with the technical cause, sending the Shipment back to Planned |
| [carrier-booking-withdrawn.v1](carrier-booking-withdrawn.v1.schema.json) | Event: the Planner withdrew a Requested Carrier booking before the Carrier answered, sending the Shipment back to Planned |
| [tracking-event.v1](tracking-event.v1.schema.json) | Command: a Tracking event a Carrier or Own fleet (a Driver, P12) reported for a Shipment, as the Carrier-tracking or Driver reports Logic App sends it to the TMS Tracking event API's record operation, naming its source |
| [proof-of-delivery.v1](proof-of-delivery.v1.schema.json) | Command: a Proof of delivery's metadata, as the Carrier Proof of delivery or Driver reports Logic App (P12) sends it in the `metadata` part of its multipart call to the TMS Proof of delivery API, naming its source and describing its files; the files travel in the `files` parts |
| [shipment-collected.v1](shipment-collected.v1.schema.json) | Event: a Tracking event moved a Booked Shipment to Collected |
| [shipment-in-transit.v1](shipment-in-transit.v1.schema.json) | Event: a Tracking event moved a Shipment to In transit |
| [shipment-delivered.v1](shipment-delivered.v1.schema.json) | Event: a Tracking event moved a Shipment to Delivered |
| [shipment-exception-reported.v1](shipment-exception-reported.v1.schema.json) | Event: an exception Tracking event was recorded for a Shipment, with the exception type and remark |
| [proof-of-delivery-received.v1](proof-of-delivery-received.v1.schema.json) | Event: a Proof of delivery was recorded as a Shipment's current one, with who received the goods, when, in which condition and how many files; never the files themselves |
| [carrier-cost-incurred.v1](carrier-cost-incurred.v1.schema.json) | Event: a Carrier Shipment moved to Delivered, so the Carrier earned the Carrier cost of its Confirmed Carrier booking, with the booking number and reference, the Shipment and Transport Order numbers, the Carrier, the Carrier cost with its charge lines and source, and the delivery time; sample messages are in [samples/carrier-cost-incurred](samples/carrier-cost-incurred) |
| [trip-dispatched.v1](trip-dispatched.v1.schema.json) | Event: a Planner dispatched a Trip, with its Vehicle, Driver, planned start and driving duration and every Stop in order, each with its Shipment and Transport Order numbers, place, window and goods lines; sample messages are in [samples/trip-dispatched](samples/trip-dispatched) |
| [trip-not-delivered.v1](trip-not-delivered.v1.schema.json) | Event: the Trip dispatch Logic App gave up sending a Trip's latest version to the Driver app, with the Trip number, version, Driver ID and technical cause; sample messages are in [samples/trip-not-delivered](samples/trip-not-delivered) |
| [trip-started.v1](trip-started.v1.schema.json) | Event: the Driver started a Dispatched Trip on its day, so it is In progress, with the Trip number, version, Driver ID and start time; sample messages are in [samples/trip-started](samples/trip-started) |
| [trip-completed.v1](trip-completed.v1.schema.json) | Event: the Driver ended an In progress Trip, so it is Completed, with the Trip number, version, Driver ID, end time and the Shipments delivered, not delivered (now Awaiting re-delivery) and not collected (now Planned); sample messages are in [samples/trip-completed](samples/trip-completed) |
| [trip-start-reported.v1](trip-start-reported.v1.schema.json) | Driver app event: the Driver pressed Start Trip on a Dispatched Trip, with the Driver app event ID, Trip number, the version the Driver app held, the Driver ID from the token and the event time; the TMS decides whether the Trip starts; sample messages are in [samples/trip-start-reported](samples/trip-start-reported) |
| [stop-completed-reported.v1](stop-completed-reported.v1.schema.json) | Driver app event: the Driver reported on a Stop (Collected, Collection failed, Delivered, Delivery failed, or Damaged, which doesn't complete it), with the Shipment, the Stop, the Tracking event type, the Stop's address as the location, the remark and the same Driver app identity keys as `trip-start-reported`; the TMS decides the Stop's outcome; sample messages are in [samples/stop-completed-reported](samples/stop-completed-reported) |
| [trip-end-reported.v1](trip-end-reported.v1.schema.json) | Driver app event: the Driver pressed End Trip on an In progress Trip whose Stops all have an outcome, with the Driver app event ID, Trip number, the version the Driver app held, the Driver ID from the token and the event time; the TMS decides whether the Trip is Completed and refuses with `StopsOpen` if a Stop is open there; sample messages are in [samples/trip-end-reported](samples/trip-end-reported) |
| [proof-of-delivery-captured.v1](proof-of-delivery-captured.v1.schema.json) | Driver app event: the Driver confirmed the Proof of delivery form at a delivery Stop, with the document ID, who received the goods, the delivery time, the Stop's address, clean or with reservations (and the remark) and the blob references of the 1 to 5 files in the Driver app's transit container (the claim check of ADR 0009), and the same Driver app identity keys as `stop-completed-reported`, whose correlation ID it shares; the TMS decides whether the Proof of delivery is recorded; sample messages are in [samples/proof-of-delivery-captured](samples/proof-of-delivery-captured) |
| [driver-report-outcome.v1](driver-report-outcome.v1.schema.json) | Command: what became of one Driver report (Recorded, Refused with the TMS's code and message, or Not recorded), as the Driver reports Logic App sends it to the Driver app's outcome endpoint; sample messages are in [samples/driver-report-outcome](samples/driver-report-outcome) |
| [shipment-taken-off-trip.v1](shipment-taken-off-trip.v1.schema.json) | Event: the Planner took a Shipment off its Trip before it was collected, with the Shipment, Transport Order and Trip numbers, the Trip's new version (none for a Draft) and the reason, sending the Shipment back to Planned; sample messages are in [samples/shipment-taken-off-trip](samples/shipment-taken-off-trip) |
| [exception-opened.v1](exception-opened.v1.schema.json) | Event: the TMS opened an Exception for a Shipment, with its Exception number, type, cause and who carries the Shipment |
| [exception-resolved.v1](exception-resolved.v1.schema.json) | Event: the Planner resolved an Exception, with its outcome and remark |
| [carrier-booking-cancelled.v1](carrier-booking-cancelled.v1.schema.json) | Event: the Planner cancelled a Confirmed Carrier booking before collection, with a reason, sending the Shipment back to Planned |
| [carrier-cancellation-not-delivered.v1](carrier-cancellation-not-delivered.v1.schema.json) | Event: the Carrier cancellation Logic App's message for a Cancelled or Withdrawn Carrier booking never reached the Carrier, with the technical cause; the booking is flagged Carrier not told |
| [shipper-notification-requested.v1](shipper-notification-requested.v1.schema.json) | Event: the Planner notified the Shipper from an open Exception, with the message, the Shipment's ETA or agreed delivery window and the notification ID |
| [shipper-notification-not-delivered.v1](shipper-notification-not-delivered.v1.schema.json) | Event: the Shipper notification Logic App's message never reached the Shipper, with the technical cause |
| [shipment-delivery-window-agreed.v1](shipment-delivery-window-agreed.v1.schema.json) | Event: the Planner agreed a delivery window with the Carrier for a Collected or In transit Shipment |
| [shipper-transport-order-list-item.v1](shipper-transport-order-list-item.v1.schema.json) | Response: the Shipper's view of one of its Transport Orders in a list, with its Tracking status, ETA and last update, as the TMS Shipper tracking API's list operation returns it; never a Carrier, a Shipment number, an Exception or Stale |
| [shipper-transport-order-detail.v1](shipper-transport-order-detail.v1.schema.json) | Response: the Shipper's view of one Transport Order, got by number or found by its Shipper reference, with its places, windows, Tracking status, ETA, last update, progress and current Proof of delivery with its files; never a Carrier, a Shipment number, an Exception, Stale or a remark |

Conventions: property names in camelCase, timestamps as UTC with an offset, weights in kilograms as numbers, money in EUR without VAT as numbers rounded to cents, countries as ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes.
